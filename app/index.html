About this guide

This is a free, open-source, offline trophy guide and tracker for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5. It covers all 55 official trophies with their names, descriptions and grades exactly as PlayStation publishes them, and a guide to each in our own words.

Where the facts come from

  • Trophy names, descriptions and grades: the official PlayStation 5 list, as listed by Exophase — the two sources agree on every name, description and grade.
  • PSN progress counts (such as 42 Shooting Range challenges): PSNProfiles.
  • Every guide method: our own advice drawn from the official description. Where the description does not pin a place or a step down, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing one — the game released on 19 November 2026 and the exact methods have not been checked in the game yet.

How the tracker works

Tick a trophy off and it stays ticked: progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and never leaves your machine. Export downloads your progress as a JSON file; import reads one back. The spoiler setting hides the names and descriptions of story trophies until you earn them. The guide loads no file from outside this folder and makes no network request — it works with the network off.

Honesty note

Four trophies have triggers the official description does not spell out — Challenging Tank, Personal Best, Bad Luck, I Guess, and The Truth Is Out There. Their guides say what the description suggests and flag the uncertainty rather than guessing a method as fact. When the community confirms those triggers, this guide should be updated to name them.
